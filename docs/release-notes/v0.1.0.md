v0.1.0 — Connect your sound

LoopZ's first preview brings application audio, microphones and hardware inputs
into named virtual devices. Route channels visually, record the resulting mix
in another app, and listen through your chosen hardware outputs.

- Sources, outputs and monitors have their own columns and live meters.
- Each virtual device has an on/off switch that preserves its wiring.
- Saved graphs reconnect when sources or devices return.
- One persisted buffer choice covers all hardware used by the mixes.
- English, Russian and Simplified Chinese controls use SingZ's language picker.
- Settings can check for updates automatically or on demand.
- The DMG contains one app with native first-run audio setup. Users do not need
  Python, Node.js or developer tools.

Apple Silicon; macOS 14.2 or newer. Driver and daemon installation requires
administrator authorization once. Microphone and application audio permissions
are controlled by macOS.

This is a preview: small-buffer source gaps remain under investigation,
and the buffer duration displayed in Settings is not total end-to-end latency.
Some diagnostics remain in English. Fresh-Mac installation and third-party app
compatibility need further acceptance testing.
